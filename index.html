No one should face sickle cell without knowledge or support.

Amara Hope Foundation raises awareness, educates families and communities, and supports people living with sickle cell disease, so fewer people have to face it alone and uninformed.

What we do

Awareness

We talk openly about sickle cell so more people understand the condition and know how to respond.

Education

We run outreaches and share clear information with families, schools and communities.

Empowerment

We support people living with sickle cell and their families, and help directly where possible.

Why we started

Hope Foundation was founded in memory of ______, who lived with sickle cell disease and passed away from its complications. Her family watched her move between hospital visits and everyday life, often meeting people, even in the medical field, who understood very little about the condition she carried. That gap between how common sickle cell is and how little is known about it is what this foundation exists to close.

We are not a hospital or a research body. We are a community effort: we explain sickle cell in plain language, go to where people already are (schools, churches, markets, community halls), and support individuals and families living with it today, with information first and practical help where we are able.
