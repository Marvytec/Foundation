Our programs and outreaches

What we do, what we have done, and what is coming next. Each program exists to close a specific gap we have seen firsthand.

Ongoing programs

Awareness campaigns

Public campaigns, social media content and community events that explain what sickle cell is, how it is inherited, and why knowing your genotype matters, aimed at young people, couples and parents who may never have had it explained clearly.

Community education

Talks, school visits and workshops with teachers, youth groups and faith communities, covering what sickle cell looks like day to day, how to recognise a pain crisis, and how classmates, coworkers and neighbours can respond with support rather than fear.

Support and empowerment

Direct support for individuals and families managing sickle cell: connecting them with medical resources and support groups and, where we are able, helping with medication costs, hospital bills or other urgent needs.

Genotype testing drives

Free or subsidised genotype testing events run with health partners, so couples and young people can know their status and make informed decisions, one of the most effective ways to prevent new cases.
